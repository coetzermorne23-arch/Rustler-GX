## Short description

Building Rustler GX — an open-source vehicle, camping and off-grid control platform combining navigation, media, vehicle telemetry, Bluetooth devices, solar, batteries and custom hardware in one interface.

## About

Rustler GX started as a hands-on project to bring the electronics I actually use into one interface.

What began around camping, Bluetooth power equipment and vehicle electronics grew into a much larger idea: a local-first platform that can connect navigation, media, vehicle telemetry, Victron equipment, fridges, tanks, sensors, relays and custom ESP32 hardware without needing a separate app for everything.

Ranger_GX grew directly from my own Ford Ranger T6/T7 head-unit build. It is becoming a dedicated driving interface with GPS, offline navigation, media controls and planned OBD-II telemetry while keeping the screen clean enough to actually use in a vehicle.

Rustler GX itself is broader than Ranger_GX. The goal is for the same core platform to work on phones, tablets, computers, Android head units and eventually Raspberry Pi-based hubs for camping and off-grid installations.

The project is being developed independently in South Africa.

Supporting me here helps pay for the real hardware needed to build and test it — OBD-II adapters, Bluetooth devices, sensors, head units and other equipment.

Supporters will also get development updates, previews, selected early/experimental builds and opportunities to help influence what gets built next.

The core project is intended to remain open source.

Thanks for helping me turn a personal project into something other people can build on and use.
