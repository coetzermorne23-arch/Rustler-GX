# Support Rustler GX

Rustler GX is developed independently in South Africa.

Development requires more than coding time. Real hardware is needed to test Bluetooth protocols, vehicle OBD-II behaviour, Android head units, power equipment and camping integrations.

You can support the project here:

**https://www.patreon.com/cw/MorneCoetzer**

## Proposed Patreon tiers

The exact pricing and benefits can evolve as the project grows.

### Supporter — R35/month

For people who simply want to help keep Rustler GX moving.

- Support development
- Patreon development posts
- Project progress updates
- Supporter polls
- Name in supporter credits when requested

### GX Builder — R75/month

For people who want to follow development more closely.

Everything in Supporter, plus:

- Early development screenshots and previews
- Early access to selected test builds
- More detailed build/dev logs
- Voting on integration priorities
- Behind-the-scenes hardware testing updates

### GX Crew — R150/month

For enthusiasts who want to actively help shape the project.

Everything in GX Builder, plus:

- Access to selected experimental builds
- Priority consideration for feature/integration suggestions
- Early testing opportunities
- GX Crew supporter credit
- Occasional technical notes on protocols and hardware experiments

### Project Sponsor — R300/month

For people or small businesses who want to make a larger contribution to development.

Everything in GX Crew, plus:

- Sponsor credit when requested
- Higher-priority consideration for compatible hardware/integration testing
- Direct acknowledgement in supporter updates
- Help fund physical test hardware

## What support pays for

Patreon income can help fund:

- OBD-II adapters
- Android head units
- Bluetooth hardware
- Victron and other supported devices
- ESP32 development hardware
- Sensors
- Test equipment
- Map/navigation development
- Hosting or development services where needed

## Open-source note

Supporting the project does not remove the freedoms granted by the license for GPL-covered code.

Patreon benefits are intended to focus on supporting development, early access, previews, community participation, experimental work and separately developed extras rather than taking away the open-source core.

Thank you for helping build Rustler GX.
